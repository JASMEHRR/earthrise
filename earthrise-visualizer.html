Animated lyrics

Each line comes in word by word out of a blur. Key words are big, filler words small, so every line reads like a poster. Lines change every two bars, on the beat.

Record visualizer

The song's picture disc spins in the centre with 120 bars around the rim. Each bar is a slice of the sound from bass to treble, mirrored left and right. The disc and glow pulse on the kick.

Running it on your real desktop with Spotify: Lively Wallpaper (free, on the Microsoft Store) can set an HTML page as your wallpaper and pass it your PC's live audio and the current song title. This page already listens for both, so the bars and title follow whatever Spotify plays. Spotify does not let web pages read its audio directly, which is why it has to come from system audio. Synced lyrics would need a lyrics source on top.
